The question

You already know you need to upskill. What will you actually become good at?

Maybe you’re still in university, about to graduate, finding your footing, or already working but know your skill set isn’t enough. You know the market is changing and AI is changing how people work.

But you’re still asking: What should I learn? Where do I start? How do I stay consistent? What can I show an employer or client?

And while you figure it out, another month passes. Another course gets saved. “I’ll start next week” becomes “next month.”

You need a focused period of execution, not necessarily more information.

I know this because I’ve done it myself.

In 2023, I went on a Graphic Design Learning Sprint. I committed, practised and kept building, and it helped me become a professional graphic designer, creating the functional designs you’re seeing here.

Then I went on another Learning Sprint and became a professional CV writer. I eventually hosted a CV Writing + Portfolio Workshop with 1,000+ participants.

A 30-day sprint doesn’t magically make you an expert. But when you stop casually consuming and focus on one capability, you learn, practise, apply, make mistakes, build and get better. Eventually you have something you didn’t have before: ability.

Now I’m doing it again, this time with AI: not to become an “AI person,” but to build real solutions, automate repetitive work and make what I’m already building even more powerful.

And this time around, I want to do it with you.

Welcome

Welcome to the 30 Days Sprint Learning Challenge.

This isn’t another course you buy, watch for three days and forget about. It’s a 30-day commitment to yourself.

You’ll choose one skill, lock in, learn deliberately, practise consistently and build something tangible.

The goal isn’t to know everything. It’s to become better at something valuable.

Who this is for

This sprint is for you if…

  • You’re a student, graduate or young professional who knows your current abilities aren’t enough.
  • You want to learn AI and use it practically.
  • You’re already learning something but need structure and accountability.
  • You’ve collected courses but haven’t turned them into practical ability.
  • You have several interests and need help choosing a focus.

Not for you if you want a magic shortcut, someone else to do the work, or just another certificate.
